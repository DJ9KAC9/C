# Higgsfield creative direction — Ora

Global style block (prepend to every prompt):
> Cinematic editorial healthcare photography, medium format look, 50mm–85mm, shallow depth of field, soft directional late-afternoon window light, warm charcoal shadows, ivory and stone surfaces, muted sage and deep emerald accents, brushed brass hardware at hairline scale, no clinical blue, no harsh overhead light, no stock smiles, no text, no logos, calm and unhurried, real skin texture, film grain 8%, color graded warm-neutral.

Negative block:
> cartoon, illustration, oversaturated, bright blue, fluorescent lighting, exaggerated smiles, gore, blood, open wounds, drills in mouths, disturbing dental close-ups, watermark, text.

1. **Homepage hero (16:9 and 9:16 loop, 8s).** Slow dolly through an empty treatment room at golden hour; a single chair in ivory leather, brass lamp arm, light moving across a stone wall. No people. The feeling of a place that has been prepared for you.
2. **Dental environment.** Wide, symmetrical frame of the reception: oak, travertine, a low sage sofa, a window with sheer linen, soft bokeh of the city beyond. One receptionist mid-motion, out of focus.
3. **Premium patient experience.** Over-the-shoulder of a patient reclined, watching a treatment plan on a wall screen, dentist gesturing calmly; patient's hand relaxed on the armrest. Faces softly lit, not posed.
4. **Specialist portraits (×4, consistent).** Three-quarter portrait, seated, dark charcoal backdrop, single soft key light from camera left, neutral expression with warmth, white linen coat, hands visible. Same lens, same crop, same light for every doctor.
5. **Skin Lounge.** A quieter room: sage walls, a single treatment bed with ivory linen, a ceramic bowl, dried botanicals, afternoon light in long stripes. One practitioner's hands preparing a tray.
6. **Cinematic treatment close-ups.** Macro of a gloved hand holding a brass-tipped instrument against stone; a drop of serum on skin; polished ceramic veneer shells on velvet. Instruments as objects of precision, never inside a mouth.
7. **Abstract brand visuals.** Slow-motion light refracting through frosted glass; ivory silk moving in air; a single ripple in water lit from the side. For section backgrounds and loading states.
8. **Social campaign (1:1, 4:5).** Series "Care, considered." Each frame: one object, one line of natural light, one accent of emerald or brass. Toothbrush on travertine. A glass of water catching sun. A folded towel on sage. Consistency over variety.
