Two rooms, ready when you are.

Both operatories run the same standard: modern chairs, intraoral camera and screen, digital X-ray, autoclave sterilization, and a stocked materials cabinet if you want it.

Two ways to pay. Pick per booking.

Choose what fits how you work. Occasional cases? Take the revenue share. Practising here regularly? The monthly residency quickly pays for itself.

Revenue share

40% of case fees to the clinic

You keep 60% of what your patient pays. The clinic provides the room, machinery, standard materials and consumables, sterilization, reception, and payment handling. Minimum 25 JOD per booking.

  • Chair, machinery, and digital X-ray
  • Standard materials and consumables from our stock
  • Sterilized instrument sets per patient
  • Reception, patient check-in, and card payments

Monthly residency

20JOD / hour

A fixed monthly rate, your own materials, 100% of your fees. Book any available hours in both rooms — Clinic 2 daytime, Clinic 1 evenings and Fridays.

  • Chair, machinery, suction, and light
  • Sterilization of your instrument sets
  • Reception and waiting area for your patients
  • Add-ons: assistant +8 JOD/h · X-ray 3 JOD/exposure · intraoral scan 15 JOD/case — both models

Rates are launch pricing and reviewed quarterly. Lab work is always billed separately at cost. First-time partners are verified once — license and one document, five minutes.

House rules

Short, and non-negotiable.

Sterility

Every set autoclaved, every surface reset.

Instrument sets are sealed and dated. Rooms are turned over between dentists by our team, never rushed between patients.

Materials

Everything from our cabinet is logged.

On the share model, take what the case needs. On monthly residency, our stock is available at list price if you run short — logged at the chair, added at check-out.

Your patients

They're yours, before and after.

We don't market to your patients or keep their clinical records. You bring them, you treat them, you follow up with them.

Cancellation

Free until 24 hours before.

Inside 24 hours, the 25 JOD share minimum applies. Emergencies happen; talk to us.
